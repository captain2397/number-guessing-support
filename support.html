How to play

Find the secret number

Every level uses a secret three-digit number from 100 through 999. Type a complete three-digit guess and tap Submit.

Follow the clues

Too High means your guess is above the secret number. Too Low means it is below. Use each clue to narrow the range before your next guess.

Use your attempts

A level starts with 10 attempts. A correct guess clears the level and awards score points. If you run out of attempts, the secret number is revealed and you can replay the level.

Hints and extra tries

A hint reveals the first digit of the secret number. Extra Try restores one used attempt, up to the normal limit of 10.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a gambling or betting app?

No. Number Guessing, including content displayed through its in-app web view, does not offer betting, deposits, withdrawals, wagering, sweepstakes, lotteries, gift cards, cash prizes, or rewards of real-world value. Scores, levels and achievements are progress markers only — they cannot be transferred, sold, withdrawn, or redeemed.

What is the in-app web view?

The app includes a web content module that can display a web page inside the app. Which page loads is determined by a remote configuration fetched at launch, so the content can change without an app update. Content shown there is governed by the terms and privacy notice of whoever operates that page. See the privacy policy for details about this module.

Do I need an account?

No. The number game requires no sign-up, no login, and no personal details.

My progress disappeared

Game progress in version 1.0 is temporary and may reset when you close or restart the app, leave the current game, or reinstall the app. Progress is not backed up to a server and cannot currently be restored.

The app is slow or crashes

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If that does not help, restart the device and confirm iOS is up to date. Still broken? Email us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I delete my data?

Number Guessing does not create an account or server-side user profile. The SDK provider confirms that the SDK does not track users or collect user data. Deleting the app removes data stored in the app's container. A random internal installation identifier stored in the iOS Keychain may remain after deletion and reinstallation; it is not used for tracking.
