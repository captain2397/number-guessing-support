How to play

Find the secret number

Every level picks a secret three-digit number from 100 through 999. Type a complete three-digit guess and tap Submit.

Follow the clues

Too High means your guess is above the secret number. Too Low means it is below. Use each clue to narrow the range before your next guess.

Use your attempts

A level starts with 10 attempts. A correct guess clears the level and awards score points. If you run out of attempts, the secret number is revealed and you can replay the level.

Hints and extra tries

A hint reveals the first digit of the secret number. Extra Try restores one used attempt, up to the normal limit of 10.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a gambling or betting app?

The number game itself is not. It has no betting, deposits, withdrawals, player-to-player wagering, sweepstakes, lotteries, gift cards, or prizes of real-world value. Scores, levels and achievements are progress markers only — they cannot be transferred, sold, withdrawn, or redeemed.

What is the in-app web view?

The app includes a web content module that can display a web page inside the app. Which page loads is determined by a remote configuration fetched at launch, so the content can change without an app update. Content shown there is governed by the terms and privacy notice of whoever operates that page. See the privacy policy for the data this module can share.

Do I need an account?

No. The number game requires no sign-up, no login, and no personal details.

My progress disappeared

Progress in the current build is held for the session and is not backed up to a server. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it. There is no restore.

The app is slow or crashes

Force-quit the app and reopen it. If that does not help, restart the device and confirm iOS is up to date. Still broken? Email us with your device model and iOS version.

How do I turn off push notifications?

Open the iOS Settings app, find Number Guessing, tap Notifications, and turn off Allow Notifications.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To request deletion of analytics, attribution or push records held by our service providers, email akash.meena2300@gmail.com from the address you contact us with and say "data deletion request". See the privacy policy for details.

Reporting a bug or requesting a feature

Email us. Bug reports with reproduction steps and a screenshot get fixed fastest.
