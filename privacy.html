Privacy Policy

Effective 13 August 2026 · Applies to Number Guessing for iPhone, version 1.0

This policy explains how Number Guessing ("the app") handles information. In short: the number game runs entirely on your device, and the app does not collect or transmit your personal or device data off your device. Questions: akash.meena2300@gmail.com.

1. The two parts of the app

The app has two distinct parts, and they behave differently:

The number game

The puzzle itself runs entirely on your device. It needs no account, asks for no personal details, and does not send your guesses, scores, or level progress anywhere.

The web content module

The app also bundles a component that can display a web page inside the app using a system web view. The address it loads is not fixed in the app: it comes from a remote configuration the app fetches at launch, so it can change without an app update. This web view is display-only — it does not accept information you enter, and the app does not pass your installation identifier or any on-device data to it. The page itself is operated by a third party under that party's own terms and privacy notice.

Please note: content displayed in the web content module is operated by a third party and is governed by that party's own terms and privacy notice, which this policy does not control.

2. What information is involved

Information stored on your device

DataPurpose
Internal installation UUID stored in the iOS KeychainIdentifies the installation so the app behaves consistently across launches. It stays on your device, is not transmitted, and — as the SDK provider has confirmed — is not used for tracking.
Local key/value storage on your deviceStores settings and state between launches. It stays on your device.

Network requests

At launch the app contacts a remote-configuration service to decide what the web content module should display, and the module then loads that web content over the network. Like any internet request, these requests reveal your device's IP address to the servers that answer them; the address is used only to deliver the response and is not retained to identify you. A remote-configuration response is data the app receives — it is not information collected from you. The number game itself works offline.

Information collected off-device

Neither Number Guessing nor its SDK provider collects or retains user data from the app. Your installation UUID and your on-device storage are never transmitted off your device, and the display-only web content module does not send anything you do in the app to its operator.

Not collected by the game

The number game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise GPS location, health data, payment card details, or government identification. The app does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not register for push notifications.

3. Third-party services

Because the app sends no user or device data off your device, it does not share your personal information with anyone. For transparency, these parties are involved in delivering the app, each under its own privacy policy:

  • The provider of the bundled remote-configuration SDK — performs remote configuration that determines which content the web content module displays. The provider has confirmed that the SDK does not track users or collect user data through the app.
  • Apple — Apple independently processes information required for App Store distribution and any diagnostics you choose to share, under Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
  • The operator of any web content loaded by the web content module, whose identity is set by the remote configuration in force at the time. The web view is display-only.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with data brokers.

4. Contacting support

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message and any attachments. We use this information only to respond to and resolve your request. Support email is handled through our email provider, Google (Gmail), and is kept only as long as needed to deal with your request and any follow-up, after which it is deleted.

5. Legal bases and controller (EEA/UK)

Through normal use, the app does not collect personal data, so no processing of app data requires a legal basis. The only personal information we receive is what you send us if you contact support (see section 4). We process that under our legitimate interest in answering your enquiry, and to take steps at your request.

For EEA/UK purposes, the data controller for support correspondence is Akash Meena, contactable at akash.meena2300@gmail.com. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

6. Retention

The installation UUID remains in the iOS Keychain on your device until it is removed (see section 7). Data stored in local key/value storage remains on your device until the app clears it or you delete the app. Because the app transmits no user or device data off your device, there is nothing about you retained off-device by the SDK provider or the remote-configuration operator. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to resolve your request and any follow-up, then deleted.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete on-device data — deleting the app removes its local key/value storage. The installation UUID is stored in the iOS Keychain; the current version of the app does not provide an in-app way to remove it, and it may survive deleting and reinstalling the app. Because the UUID never leaves your device, no one else holds a copy of it to delete.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) — since the app collects no personal data through normal use, this applies mainly to any support correspondence we hold. Email akash.meena2300@gmail.com with the subject "privacy request" and we will respond within 30 days. California residents will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The web content module is display-only and collects nothing from any user. We take steps to ensure that remotely loaded content is appropriate for the app's App Store age rating. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete that correspondence.

9. Security

All connections between the app and the services it contacts — remote configuration and web content — use encrypted HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Connectivity

Remote configuration and web content require a network connection. The number game itself works offline.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.
