Privacy Policy

Effective 8 August 2026 · Applies to Number Guessing for iOS, version 1.0

This policy explains what Number Guessing ("the app") collects, why, who it is shared with, and what you can do about it. Questions: akash.meena2300@gmail.com.

1. The two parts of the app

The app has two distinct parts, and they behave differently:

The number game

The puzzle itself runs entirely on your device. It needs no account, asks for no personal details, and does not send your guesses, scores, or level progress anywhere.

The web content module

The app also bundles a component that can display a web page inside the app using a system web view. The address it loads is not fixed in the app: it comes from a remote configuration the app fetches at launch, so it can change without an app update. When that page is shown, the app can pass it the identifiers listed in section 2 through an in-app bridge, and anything you type into that page goes to whoever operates it — not to the game.

Please note: content displayed in the web content module is operated by a third party and is governed by that party's own terms and privacy notice, which this policy does not control. Do not enter passwords, payment details, or government ID numbers into web content shown inside the app.

The number game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise GPS location, health data, payment card details, or government identification.

3. Third-party services

The app shares the data above with the following processors, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Adjust — install attribution. adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Apple — App Store distribution and, if enabled by you, crash and usage reports. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • The operator of any web content loaded by the web content module, whose identity is set by the remote configuration in force at the time.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with data brokers.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Legitimate interests — keeping the app working, security, and basic usage measurement.
  • Consent — push notifications, app tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency), and any analytics or advertising identifiers used for marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time in iOS Settings.

5. Retention

Analytics and attribution records are kept by the providers above for as long as their default retention windows allow (typically 2 to 14 months, per the provider's settings) and are then deleted or aggregated. Push tokens are retained until you disable notifications or delete the app. Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off tracking — iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Turn off notifications — iOS Settings → Number Guessing → Notifications.
  • Delete everything on device — delete the app.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, or objection (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) — email akash.meena2300@gmail.com with the subject "privacy request". We respond within 30 days. California residents will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, email us and we will delete it. Because the web content module can display third-party content that we do not author, we recommend supervision for younger users.

8. Security

Traffic between the app and its service providers uses encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

The service providers listed above process data on servers in the United States and other countries. Transfers out of the EEA/UK rely on the providers' Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the effective date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app or on this page before they take effect.
